# Code of conduct

PatchHive contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, specific,
and focused on the work.

Welcome good-faith questions, differing experience levels, and evidence-backed
disagreement. Do not use harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, sexualized attention, doxxing, or sustained disruption.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Include relevant links and context. The
maintainer may edit or remove abusive content, close conversations, restrict
participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the project.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
retaliation against reporters acting in good faith.
